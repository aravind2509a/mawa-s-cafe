Our Story

A Little Cafe With A Big Appetite

Mawa's Big Belly Cafe was born from a simple belief: Hyderabad deserves a late-night refuge where the momos are crispy, the cheese is molten, and the conversations can linger past midnight without any rush.

Tucked right beside Konak Diagnostics in New Malakpet, we serve comforting fusion snacks, authentic spiced wraps, hot skillet plates, and creamy beverages in an intimate, string-lit room designed for belly laughs and big bites.
